Support

Last updated 18 November 2026

Need help with Saint Chris? Email help@saintchris.app and include your username. We aim to reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I earn a stamp? Log a destination in a country you have visited with 1 to 3 photos you took there with your camera. If they pass the check, that country is stamped.

Why was my destination not approved? The check could not match the photos to the place, found no camera information in them, or saw that a photo had been used before. Open the record, read the tip, then edit and resubmit with a fresh photo taken at the place.

Can I use photos from my camera roll? Yes, if they are original camera photos. Pictures saved from the internet or messaging apps are usually rejected because they carry no camera information.

Why one stamp per country? Stamps are for countries. More places in a country are kept in your records without adding a stamp.

Who can see my photos and comments? You decide in Menu, Settings. By default photos are shown to travellers you have added.

How do messages work? You can message any traveller. Until they add you back, your message shows as a request without its text, and you can send a few before they reply.

How do I turn notifications off? Menu, Settings, Notifications. You can also change this in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I delete my account? Menu, Delete my account. Everything you own is removed.

How do I report someone? Open their profile in Travellers and tap Report or Block.
