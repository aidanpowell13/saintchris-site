Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Saint Chris is a social travel passport. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how you can delete it. If anything is unclear, write to help@saintchris.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our login provider), or your Apple sign-in identifier, which may be a private relay email address from Apple.
  • Profile: your username, passport look, optional tagline and home base, your stamps and the places listed under them, notes you write, and your dreams (places you want to visit).
  • Photos: up to three photos for each destination you submit for a stamp, stored in private storage.
  • Camera information and photo fingerprints: to check that a photo was taken with a camera and has not been used before, your device keeps only the camera make, model, editing software and capture time from the photo's metadata. Location data (GPS) is removed. It also creates a short numeric fingerprint of each photo so that the same picture cannot be reused for several stamps.
  • Messages and comments: direct messages you send and receive, and comments you write on destinations and dreams.
  • Connections and safety: the travellers you add, the people you block, reports you make or receive, and how many photo checks you run each day (to prevent abuse).
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token that identifies your phone to Apple's notification service, and your on or off choice.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in can see your username, passport look, tagline, home base, stamped countries, the places listed under them, and your dreams.
  • Photos of an approved destination are visible only to travellers you have added by default. In Settings you can change this to everyone or nobody. Photos of destinations that were not approved are never visible to others.
  • Comments on your destinations and dreams can be limited in Settings to everyone, travellers you have added, or nobody. People who have blocked you or been blocked by you never see each other's content.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you are writing to. A message sent to someone who has not added you appears as a request without its text until they add you.
  • Your email address and your destination records are never shown to other users.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase stores our database, sign-in records, private photos and server functions.
  • Anthropic (the company behind Claude) receives your submitted photos, sent from our server, to judge whether they show the place and look original. We do not use them for advertising.
  • Google Cloud Vision may receive the same photos from our server so that it can tell us whether an exact copy already appears on the web. It is used only for that check.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and push notifications. Expo forwards push notifications to Apple. Notifications contain the sender's username and, for messages from travellers you have added, the start of the message.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Deleting a destination removes its photos. Deleting your account removes your profile, stamps, dreams, photos, messages, comments and connections from our systems. Copies may remain in routine backups for a limited time.

Your choices

  • Delete any destination, dream or comment at any time.
  • Delete your whole account in the app: Menu, Delete my account.
  • Control who sees your photos and comments, and turn notifications off, in Settings.
  • Block or report another traveller from their profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or for corrections by email.

Children

Saint Chris is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
